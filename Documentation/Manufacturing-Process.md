# Manufacturing Process

This is a preliminary workflow, not a record of completed manufacturing. Adapt it to the verified drawings, materials, workshop procedures, and qualified operator requirements.

1. **Complete the 3D model:** Confirm units and datums; resolve the drum, frame, shaft, bearing, crank, lid, fit, and clearance dimensions in the [project dimension register](Project-Dimensions.md).
2. **Release manufacturing information:** Prepare dimensioned drawings, a frame cut list, material specifications, and interface details. Identify tolerances only where required by the design.
3. **Prepare and fixture the frame:** Mark, cut, and deburr specified members; check the A-frame and brace layout, diagonals, squareness, and stability before joining.
4. **Fabricate and inspect:** Weld or fasten only as shown in approved drawings and using suitable workshop procedures. Record as-fabricated dimensions and inspect joints before assembly.
5. **Finish the frame:** Remove sharp edges and apply an appropriate corrosion-protective finish after inspection.
6. **Assemble rotational supports:** Install bearing blocks and bushes; check alignment, retention, free movement, and serviceability.
7. **Fit drum, hub, and crank:** Follow the assembly drawing, verify retention, and check the drum and crank clearances without applying a load.
8. **Fit the folding lid:** Check hinge movement and positive closure before any rotation test.
9. **Record final assembly dimensions:** Measure the assembled envelope, support footprint, axis height, interfaces, and moving clearances; document deviations from CAD.
10. **Conduct a controlled mechanical inspection:** Check stability, clearances, fasteners, pinch points, and unloaded rotation before any safely planned test.
11. **Document the build:** Add actual fabrication photographs and record inspection results, test conditions, and corrective actions.

Team-supplied photographs and SolidWorks files are now included in the repository. Photos show frame-welding work and a red-drum model on its support frame; the CAD correspondence, fabrication completion, safe operation, and test results remain unverified. This preliminary sequence is not a record that each step has been completed.
