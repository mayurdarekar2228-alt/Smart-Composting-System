# Mechanical Design

## Arrangement

The intended system uses a 20-litre drum mounted horizontally in a fabricated metal frame. A hand-operated crank applies rotation. Bearings and bushes support the rotating arrangement, and a folding lid provides access for loading. An A-frame and longitudinal bracing are named in the supplied part list.

## Design checks

Before fabrication or use, resolve these items in the assembly and manufacturing drawings:

- Drum and support dimensions, mass, center of gravity, and safe working load.
- Shaft, hub, bearing, and bush interfaces, fits, alignment, retention, and service access.
- Frame member sizes, materials, weld or fastener details, corrosion protection, and stability.
- Crank length, required hand force, rotating clearance, and pinch-point guarding.
- Folding lid hinge, latch, and secure closure while the drum is turned.
- Access for filling, emptying, cleaning, inspection, and bearing/bush maintenance.
- Drum rotation stops or other means to prevent uncontrolled motion during loading.

The supplied SolidWorks assembly and part files are available in [`../assets/cad/`](../assets/cad/). A verified dimensioned drawing and as-built measurement record are not included, so dimensions and interfaces remain to be confirmed. See the [project dimension register](Project-Dimensions.md); the listed part names in [Components](Components.md) are not a substitute for released fabrication drawings.

This is a manual-only concept. No motor, electrical supply, control system, sensor, or IoT function is part of the specified design.
